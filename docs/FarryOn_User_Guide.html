Setup & configuration for the FarryOn Android app Version 1.0 · Everything you can set up, step by step

Before you begin — how settings work

FarryOn is a voice-first AI assistant for Android. You talk, and Farry listens, sees through your phone camera or smart glasses, remembers, and gets things done.

All configuration lives in one place: the Settings screen (tap the gear icon). Settings are grouped into sections — Assistant, Devices, Connections, Your stuff, Account, About.

Important — "Save & reconnect". Most settings pages have a Save & reconnect button pinned at the bottom. Your changes only take effect after you tap it — Farry briefly reconnects to apply them. A few quick toggles (like capture devices) apply instantly and have no Save button.

1. Getting started

1.1 Install the app

  1. Open the FarryOn website on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. When prompted, allow installing apps from your browser (Android → Install unknown apps).
  3. Open the downloaded APK, tap Install, then Open.

1.2 Set the server address (only if asked to)

Most users can skip this — the app ships pointed at the FarryOn cloud. Change it only if you were given a different server. 1. On the Sign In screen, tap the small host : port chip at the bottom (globe icon). 2. In the Server address sheet, enter Host, Port, and turn Use TLS (https/wss) on or off. 3. Tap Save.

1.3 Create an account

  1. On the welcome screen tap Create Account.
  2. Fill in First name and Last name (optional), Email, and Password. - Password must be at least 8 characters.
  3. Tap Sign Up. You are signed in automatically. - Or tap Continue with Google (shown only if Google sign-in is enabled for your build).

1.4 Sign in

  1. Tap Sign In, enter your Email and Password (use the eye icon to reveal it), tap Sign In.
  2. Google: tap Continue with Google if available.
  3. Two-factor (2FA): if your account has 2FA on, after your password you'll see Two-factor check — enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (or a recovery code) and tap Verify Code.

1.5 Grant permissions

FarryOn asks for permissions only when a feature needs them (see the full list in Section 15). Tap Allow when prompted so voice, camera, glasses, reminders and contacts work. If you accidentally deny one, you can re-enable it from Android Settings → Apps → FarryOn → Permissions.

2. Connecting your smart glasses (L801 / L802)

Both the L801 and L802 connect the same way — the app finds them over Bluetooth by name, so these steps apply to either model.

2.1 Before you start

  • Power the glasses on and keep them near your phone.
  • Turn Bluetooth on.
  • When asked, grant the Nearby devices permission (Android 12+). On older phones this uses Location instead.

2.2 Connect

  1. Go to Settings → Devices → Glasses.
  2. The app scans for a few seconds: - One pair found → it connects automatically. - Several found → a Choose glasses list appears. Each entry shows Paired with this phone, Nearby (signal strength), or Not seen nearby — may be off, with its address. Tap the one you want. - Nothing found → you'll see "No glasses found — make sure they are on and nearby." Check they're powered on and retry.
  3. Once connected, the Glasses tile shows Connected · NN% battery. The app remembers this pair and auto-reconnects next time.

Tip. If the glasses are already busy/connected they may not show up in a scan even though they still work — the app will tell you rather than hide them.

2.3 Glasses speaker volume

  1. Go to Settings → Devices → Glasses volume.
  2. Drag the slider (0–100%). This controls the glasses' own speaker, separate from your phone volume.
  3. It's saved and re-applied every time you connect — you can even set it while disconnected; it takes effect on the next connect.

2.4 Disconnect

  1. Settings → Devices → Glasses (while connected) → tap the tile.
  2. Confirm Disconnect. The glasses stay disconnected until you connect again.

Glasses Lab (Settings → About → Glasses Lab) is a hardware test bench that appears only in developer/debug builds. Regular users won't see it.

3. Choosing your capture devices (mic & camera)

Pick where Farry listens and looks from. Microphone and camera are chosen independently.

  1. Go to Settings → Devices → Capture devices.
  2. Microphone — choose one: - Phone / earbuds mic (default). - Glasses mic (long-press to talk) — requires glasses connected.
  3. Camera — choose one: - Phone camera (live, 1 fps) (default). - Glasses camera — say "what is this?" or tap the 📷 button. Requires glasses connected.
  4. Your choice applies immediately — there's no Save button here.

4. Video recording & syncing media (glasses)

  1. Go to Settings → Devices → Video recording.
  2. Clip length — choose 30 seconds / 1 minute / 2 minutes / 4 minutes (4 min is the maximum). Start a clip with the 📹 button or by saying "record a video"; the glasses stop automatically at your chosen length.
  3. Copying photos & videos to your phone — choose: - Automatically (default). - Only when I ask.
  4. Sync now — pulls any waiting photos/videos off the glasses. It's tappable only when the glasses are connected and something is waiting.

Good to know. During a transfer the glasses can't record or take photos. A 4-minute clip is a few hundred MB. Files are saved to DCIM/FarryOn on your phone. The glasses must be off the charger to transfer. Recording several clips? Set Only when I ask and sync once at the end.

5. Voice & mic mode

  1. Go to Settings → Assistant → Voice & mic.
  2. Toggle Hands-free mic: - On (default) — Always listening. Best in a quiet room; Farry detects when you start and stop talking. - Off — Tap-to-talk. The mic stays closed until you tap it. Best around background noise or a TV, so stray sounds don't trigger Farry.
  3. Tap Save & reconnect.

6. AI model

  1. Go to Settings → Assistant → AI model.
  2. Choose a provider: - Gemini ⚡ (default) — fast, best value. - OpenAI ⚡ — premium. - Mock — offline test mode (no real AI).
  3. Tap Save & reconnect.

7. Languages

  1. Go to Settings → Assistant → Languages.
  2. Pick a Primary and a Secondary language from the grids. Available: English, Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Bengali, Punjabi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Spanish, French.
  3. Tap Save & reconnect.

Farry always replies in whatever language you speak. These two are just tie-breakers for short or mixed sentences, and for messages Farry starts itself (like reminders) — primary wins. Picking the same language the other slot holds simply swaps them.

8. Email setup (up to 2 mailboxes)

Farry can read, summarize and send email by voice — for example a personal Gmail and your official / work email (any provider). You can connect up to 2 mailboxes.

8.1 First, get an app password

Most providers (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Hostinger…) require an app-specific password — not your normal login password. - Gmail: turn on 2-Step Verification, then create an App Password (16 digits) in your Google Account security settings. You'll paste that into FarryOn. - Other providers: create an app password (or use the mailbox password if your provider allows it) from your mail account's security settings.

8.2 Add a mailbox

  1. Go to Settings → Connections → Email inbox.
  2. Tap Add account.
  3. Fill in: - Label — e.g. Personal or Work (defaults to "Email" if left blank). - Provider — tap Gmail, Outlook / 365, Yahoo, Hostinger, or Custom. - Email address — e.g. you@example.com. - App password — paste the app password from step 8.1 (Gmail = 16 digits). - Custom only: if you chose Custom, also enter IMAP host (incoming), SMTP host (outgoing) and Port (default 587). Preset providers fill these in for you.
  4. Tap Test connection — a green "Connected · inbox reachable" means it works. A red message means wrong password, unreachable host, or a network problem — fix and retry.
  5. Set as primary — turn on if this should be Farry's default mailbox. (If it's your only mailbox, it's always primary.)
  6. Tap Save & reconnect.

8.3 Add a second mailbox / set the default

  • Repeat 8.2 to add a second mailbox (maximum 2). To add more you must remove one first.
  • Exactly one mailbox is the primary. Farry reads and sends from the primary unless you name the other by its label — e.g. "check my work email."
  • If you don't say which account when sending, Farry asks, so a reply never leaves the wrong address.

8.4 Edit or remove a mailbox

  1. Settings → Connections → Email inbox → tap the mailbox card.
  2. Change any field, or tap Remove account (red).
  3. Tap Save & reconnect.

Security. Your app password is stored in the phone's secure keystore, never in plain settings.

9. Web search

  1. Go to Settings → Connections → Web search.
  2. Choose a Provider: Tavily (default), Serper, or SerpAPI.
  3. API key — leave blank to use the server default, or paste your own key.
  4. Fallback API key (optional) — used automatically when your primary key runs out of free credits.
  5. Tap Save & reconnect.

Web searches count against your plan's search quota (see Section 12).

10. WhatsApp, Telegram & SMS (voice-driven)

These are hands-free, voice-driven — there is nothing to set up inside FarryOn (no accounts or keys in the app). You just speak, Farry prepares the message, and your normal messaging app opens with it ready to send.

10.1 WhatsApp

  1. Make sure WhatsApp is installed and signed in on your phone, and grant FarryOn the Contacts permission (so a spoken name finds the right number).
  2. Say, for example, "WhatsApp Ali: running late."
  3. Farry confirms the recipient and message, then opens WhatsApp with the text pre-filled — you tap Send once.

10.2 SMS

  1. Say "text Sara: on my way."
  2. Your Messages app opens with the text ready — tap Send.

10.3 Telegram

  1. Say "Telegram Ahmed: call me."
  2. What happens depends on how Telegram delivery is set up on the FarryOn server: - Direct send — if enabled, Farry sends it for you. - Bot send — Farry can message anyone who has first sent /start to the FarryOn Telegram bot. Ask your contact to do that once. - Otherwise — Farry copies the message to your clipboard and opens the Telegram chat; you paste and send (Telegram links can't pre-fill text).

WhatsApp/SMS/Telegram need no API keys from you. Contacts permission is what lets a spoken name become the right number, all on your device.

11. Your stuff — Notes, Reminders & Conversations

You add these by voice; the screens are for viewing and tidying up. Open them from Settings → Your stuff (or the top bar on the main screen).

11.1 Notes (things Farry remembered)

  • Settings → Your stuff → Notes. These are things Farry saved when you said "remember…" — its memory of your preferences and facts.
  • Add: say "remember I park on level 3."
  • Delete: swipe a note and confirm.
  • Refresh: pull down.

11.2 Reminders

  • Settings → Your stuff → Reminders. Grouped into Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later.
  • Add: say "remind me to call the bank at 5."
  • Mark done: tap the check circle.
  • Delete: swipe and confirm.
  • For reminders to fire on time, allow Notifications, Exact alarms, and the battery-optimization exemption when asked.

11.3 Conversations

  • Settings → Your stuff → Conversations. Your past chats, newest first — tap one to read the full transcript.
  • Delete one: swipe and confirm. Clear all: top-right (permanent).
  • Chats are saved on your phone when you end a session.

12. Subscription & plans

  1. Go to Settings → Account → Subscription.
  2. You'll see: - Your current plan and price. - Usage meters — Voice time, Image scans, Web searches — each showing used vs. your cap (or Unlimited, or Not included in this plan). - Upgrade options with prices.
  3. To upgrade, tap a higher plan.

Note. Online payments may not be switched on yet — upgrade tiles can show "Coming soon." They'll become tappable once payments are enabled.

13. Advanced — server address (Cloud vs Local)

  1. Go to Settings → Connections → Server.
  2. The top banner shows your live connection and the server it's using.
  3. Tap a preset, or enter values manually: - Cloud — Always online (the FarryOn cloud, TLS on). Recommended. - Local (Dev) — Your PC on Wi-Fi (for developers running the backend themselves). - Manual — set Host, Port, and the Secure (TLS) switch yourself.
  4. Tap Save & reconnect.

14. About & debug

Settings → About shows: - Debug logs — view or share Farry's tool + error trail (handy when reporting a problem). - Version — your app version. - Glasses Lab — appears in developer builds only.

15. Permissions FarryOn uses

Permission Why it's needed When it's asked
Microphone Hearing your voice When you start a session
Camera "See & Ask" / photos When you start a session
Location "Where am I?" answers; glasses scan on older phones On first location use
Nearby devices (Bluetooth) Finding & connecting glasses On glasses scan
Nearby Wi-Fi Syncing glasses photos/videos On media sync (Android 13+)
Notifications + Exact alarms Reminders firing on time On first reminder
Battery-optimization exemption Reminders not delayed by power saving On first reminder
Contacts Turning a spoken name into a number (WhatsApp/SMS/Telegram) On first message-by-name

You can review or change all of these in Android Settings → Apps → FarryOn → Permissions.

16. Signing out

  1. Go to Settings → Account → Sign out.
  2. Confirm Sign out. This ends the session on this device; you'll sign in again next time.

17. Quick troubleshooting

Problem Try this
A setting didn't apply Tap Save & reconnect at the bottom of the page.
Glasses won't connect Power them on, keep them near, allow Nearby devices, then retry the scan.
Email won't connect Use an app password (not your login password); tap Test connection; check the address/host.
Reminders don't fire Allow Notifications, Exact alarms, and the battery-optimization exemption.
A message name isn't found Grant the Contacts permission.
Can't upgrade a plan Payments may not be enabled yet ("Coming soon").
Farry hears the TV Turn Hands-free mic off (tap-to-talk) in Voice & mic.
