FAQ

The Essentials plan works with any Bluetooth glasses or headset — FarryOn pairs as a standard audio device. For Camera-to-Action features you need camera-equipped glasses like L801 or L802. Any BT 5.0+ glasses with HFP and A2DP profiles work for all voice features immediately.
Yes — Arabic (Gulf dialect), Hindi, Urdu, and English are natively supported. Real-time translation covers 25+ languages and you can switch mid-conversation. Many UAE customers use it for English ↔ Arabic in business meetings.
Siri and Google Assistant react to single questions. FarryOn is a persistent agent — it learns your patterns, remembers context across days, proactively alerts you, and executes multi-step tasks like "tell me what this landmark is, set a reminder, and message the details to a friend." All hands-free through glasses.
FarryOn processes audio only when you actively trigger it. The camera captures only on your command — no continuous recording. Your personal memory is encrypted and stored only on your account. We never sell or share user data.
Both L801 and L802 deliver approximately 8 hours of mixed use. Camera recording in continuous mode reduces this to about 90 minutes. In light voice-command use, they comfortably last a full workday. Full charge in about 60 minutes via magnetic charging.
