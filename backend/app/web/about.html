About FarryOn

Glasses that see.
An assistant that acts.

FarryOn is a startup in Dubai building a new kind of everyday tool: a pair of glasses with a camera and microphones, and an AI assistant inside them that looks at what you're looking at, listens to what you say, and gets it done — without you reaching for a phone.

The idea

Every assistant so far has lived in a phone. You take it out, unlock it, open something, type or tap, and put it back. Multiply that by every small question in a day and it adds up to a lot of reaching.

FarryOn starts from the other end. If the assistant can already see what you see and hear what you say, most of those steps disappear. Point at a menu in Arabic and hear it in Hindi. Ask what the building in front of you is. Say "message Ahmed I'm ten minutes late" and it's sent. Say "remind me at six" and it will.

That is the whole product — one motion, ask and done — and everything we build is in service of making that motion as short as possible.

Hardware and software, built together

FarryOn is both halves: the glasses you wear and the assistant that runs in them. Designing the two side by side is what makes the experience feel like one thing rather than a gadget talking to an app.

The glasses

L801, L802, Farry-GS4 & GS5 MAX

Four frames, one assistant. Every model has an 8MP camera at eye level, noise-reducing microphones, speakers aimed at your ears, Bluetooth 5.4 and Wi-Fi transfer to the app — from the everyday L801 to the GS5 MAX, with its 3000 mAh charging case and a choice of black, red or cream.

The assistant

The FarryOn app and the AI behind it

Live voice and vision, so it answers while you're still looking. Tools it can actually use — messages, email, notes, reminders, search. And a memory, so it gets more useful the longer you have it.

What it does today

  • See & ask. Landmarks, products, plants, signs, documents — "what is this?" answered aloud.
  • Live translation across 20+ languages, both directions, mid-conversation. English, Arabic, Hindi and Urdu are native.
  • WhatsApp and Telegram by voice — dictate, confirm, sent.
  • Email by voice — read, summarise and reply across your accounts.
  • Notes, reminders and tasks captured as you speak, organised for you.
  • Web search that reads you a clear answer instead of ten links.
  • Personal memory — your preferences, habits and contacts, so you don't repeat yourself.

You don't need our glasses to start

The FarryOn app pairs with any Bluetooth glasses or headset for everything voice — translation, messaging, notes, reminders, email, search. The camera features need camera-equipped glasses like the L801, L802, Farry-GS4 or GS5 MAX. So the honest way to begin is the free trial on whatever you already own, and the glasses when you've decided the assistant has earned a place in your day.

How we treat what you say and see

  • The microphone streams only while you're in a session, and the camera captures only when you ask it to. There is no continuous recording.
  • We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • What you save — notes, reminders, contacts — stays until you delete it or close your account.
  • FarryOn uses AI models, and AI can be wrong. Check anything that matters before you act on it.

The full detail is in our privacy policy, written to be read.

Where we are

FarryOn is based in Dubai and ships to the UAE and GCC. Every pair comes with a 30-day return guarantee, and the app comes with minutes of talk time free. If you have a question that isn't answered on this site, a real person answers on WhatsApp.

We're a young company building something that didn't exist before, and we'd rather tell you exactly what it does today than promise what it might do next year. What you read on this site is what ships.
